Delivery policy

Where we deliver, when it arrives, and what it costs.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Same morning, not a courier

We are a local fish stall, not a parcel service. Everything is caught overnight, landed in the morning, cleaned and cut to your order, and delivered to you the same morning on ice. We do not ship fish by courier, by post, or outside our delivery area, and we do not deliver to other states.

Where we deliver

We deliver to Panaji, Taleigao, Dona Paula and Porvorim.

If your address turns out to be outside the area after you have ordered, we will call you and either arrange pickup at the stall or refund the order in full.

When it arrives

You pick a time slot at checkout and we deliver within it. Everything is delivered the same morning, in the slot chosen at checkout.

Delivery slots:

  • 7:00 – 8:00 AM
  • 8:00 – 9:00 AM
  • 9:00 – 10:00 AM
  • 10:00 – 11:00 AM
  • 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Orders close at 10:30 AM each morning. Anything ordered after that is for the next day's catch.

On a busy morning, in heavy rain, or if a road is blocked, a delivery can run late. We will message you if it does. If we are going to miss your slot badly, you can ask for a full refund instead.

What delivery costs

  • Home delivery: ₹40 per order.
  • Free delivery on orders of ₹999 or more.
  • Stall pickup: free.

The fee is shown in your bill before you pay — there is nothing extra to pay on delivery.

Picking up at the stall

Choose "pick up at stall" at checkout and collect your order from Stall 12, Fish Market, Panaji in the slot you picked. Please bring your order ID.

Pickup slots:

  • 7:00 – 9:00 AM
  • 9:00 – 11:00 AM
  • 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Orders not collected by the end of the day cannot be held over — fresh fish does not keep. See our refunds policy for what happens then.

Receiving your order

  • Please give a complete address with a landmark, and a phone number that is switched on that morning.
  • Somebody needs to be there to take it — we cannot leave fish at the door.
  • Check the order when it arrives. If anything looks wrong, tell the delivery person straight away.
  • Put it in the fridge as soon as you get it, and cook it the same day.

Questions

Anything unclear about delivery? Ask us on the contact page before you order — we would rather answer first than refund later.
