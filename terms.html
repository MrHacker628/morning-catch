Terms & conditions

The rules for booking fish on this website. By placing an order you agree to them.

Last updated 30 October 2026

1. Who you are buying from

This website is run by Fernandes Fresh Fish, Rajesh Fernandes, Stall 12, Municipal Fish Market, Panaji, Goa 403001, India. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean that business, and "you" means the person placing the order.

2. What we sell

We sell fresh fish, prawns and crab landed the same morning. Because this is a natural product caught at sea, no two days are the same: sizes, cuts and availability change daily, and the fish shown on the site is only what is actually in the ice box that morning.

Weights are as accurate as we can make them on the scale, but fish is cleaned and cut by hand. The weight you receive may differ slightly from the weight you booked. Where the difference is more than about 10%, we will tell you before delivery and refund or adjust the difference.

3. Booking and the daily cutoff

Orders for a given morning close at 10:30 AM. After that the site stops taking bookings until the next day's catch is listed. We may also close bookings earlier if the boats do not come in, if the weather is bad, or if everything has sold out.

An order is confirmed only once your payment succeeds and you see the order ID on screen. Until then, nothing is held for you.

4. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and include all applicable taxes.
  • Prices are set per kilogram or per piece, as shown on each item.
  • Prices change daily with the market. The price you pay is the price shown when you pay.
  • A delivery fee of ₹40 applies to home delivery, free on orders of ₹999 or more. There is no fee for stall pickup.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets). We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.

5. If something is not available

Occasionally a boat lands less than expected, or an item sells out between your order and packing. If we cannot supply something you have paid for, we will contact you and either refund that item in full or, with your agreement, replace it with something similar of the same value. See our refunds and cancellations policy.

6. Delivery and pickup

Delivery areas, time slots and pickup details are set out in our delivery policy. Please make sure the address and phone number you give us are correct and that someone can receive the order in the slot you choose — fresh fish cannot wait at the door.

7. Food safety and your responsibility

We clean, cut and pack the fish on ice the same morning. Once it reaches you, please refrigerate it immediately and cook it the same day. We cannot take responsibility for fish that has been left out of refrigeration after delivery.

If you have a seafood allergy, please do not rely on this site — our fish is handled in a shared market space and cross-contact between species is possible.

8. Cancellations and refunds

Fresh fish is perishable, so cancellation is only possible within a short window. The full rules, including what happens if you are unhappy with the quality, are in our refunds and cancellations policy.

9. Your account details and misuse

Please do not place fake or repeated orders you do not intend to collect, use someone else's payment details, or try to break, scrape or overload this website. We may refuse service to anyone doing so.

10. Limits on what we are responsible for

We will always try to put a genuine problem right. Beyond that, our responsibility for any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. We are not responsible for losses outside our reasonable control — rough seas, fishing bans, strikes, power or internet failures, or a payment provider being down.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the business changes. The version on this page at the time you place an order is the one that applies to it.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts of Goa have jurisdiction over any dispute.

13. Contact

Questions about these terms? See our contact page — WhatsApp is the quickest way to reach us.
